Common Questions

How do I delete my account and all my data?

In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything tied to it — workouts, food logs, weight history, all of it — and can't be undone. See our Privacy Policy for the full details on what's deleted.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

In the app: Profile → Manage Subscription opens Apple's own subscription management sheet, where you can change plans or cancel anytime. Your subscription is billed and managed entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details.

I got a new phone — how do I get my subscription back?

Sign in with the same account, then in the app: Profile → Restore Purchases. This re-syncs your subscription from your Apple ID; no need to pay again if it's already active.

BlobFit isn't reading my steps from Apple Health

Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → BlobFit and make sure Step Count access is turned on. iOS keeps Health permissions in this separate location rather than the app's own permission list.
