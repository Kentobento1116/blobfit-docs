1.Information We Collect

Account information

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive whatever Apple provides based on your choices there (your name, and either your real email or Apple's private relay address). Your session is managed by Supabase Auth; we never see or store a password, since Sign in with Apple doesn't use one.

Health & fitness data

This is the core of what BlobFit tracks, and we treat it as sensitive:

  • Daily step count (read from Apple Health, with your permission)
  • Body weight and weight history
  • Workout sessions, exercises, sets, reps, and weight lifted
  • Food and nutrition logs, including calories and macros
  • Water intake
  • Manually logged activities (e.g. a run or bike ride you tell us about)

Meal photos

If you use the "scan a meal photo" feature, that photo is sent (as a one-time request, not stored by us afterward) to OpenAI's vision model to estimate nutrition information. This only happens after you give permission in the app when it first asks to share data with AI services, and you can withdraw that permission anytime in Profile. See Third Parties below.

Usage & diagnostic data

We use TelemetryDeck for basic, anonymized usage analytics (e.g. which screens are opened, whether a feature is used) so we know what to improve. Your health and fitness data is never included in analytics events — that's a hard rule in how the app is built, not just a policy statement.

Content you create

Workout templates and programs, custom exercises, saved meals, and any food items you enter manually.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality — tracking your workouts, nutrition, and progress over time, and syncing it across your devices
  • To power AI-assisted features you choose to use (meal photo scanning, activity MET estimation, AI food lookup)
  • To maintain your account and process subscription entitlements
  • To understand aggregate feature usage and fix problems
  • To communicate with you about your account (e.g. a trial-ending reminder), if you've enabled notifications
  • Apple HealthKit data: information read from Apple Health (such as step count) is used exclusively to display your fitness progress within the app. We never sell Apple Health data, share it with third parties, or use it for advertising, marketing, or user profiling.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your health or fitness data for advertising, and BlobFit doesn't show ads.

AI services (OpenAI and Exa)

Before any data is sent to OpenAI or Exa, BlobFit shows a screen explaining what is sent and to whom, and asks for your permission. Nothing is sent unless you choose Allow. Specifically:

  • Meal photo scan — the photo you take is sent to OpenAI.
  • Custom activity estimate — the activity name you type is sent to OpenAI.
  • AI food search — the food name you type is sent to Exa.

Your name, email, weight, workout history, and food log are never included in these requests. The data is used only to produce the estimate you asked for. We only use AI providers whose terms provide the same or equal protection for your data as this policy describes, including not using API-submitted data to train their models. You can turn AI data sharing off anytime in Profile → AI & Privacy; the rest of the app keeps working, and you can still log food manually or by barcode.

4.Data Storage & Security

  • All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS — no plaintext requests, anywhere in the app
  • Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security enabled on every table, enforced by the database itself — your account can only ever read or write its own rows, not anyone else's
  • Your authentication token is stored in the iOS Keychain, never in plain app storage
  • API keys for AI features (OpenAI, Exa) are never embedded in the app itself — they're held server-side, behind functions that verify you're a genuinely signed-in user before processing a request

5.Your Choices & Rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account, inside the app. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data — workouts, food logs, weight history, everything. It's irreversible, and we mean permanently: your data is not archived or retained after deletion.
  • Health data access: control BlobFit's access to Apple Health anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  • Sign out: available in Profile — clears your data from that device without deleting your account.
  • AI features require your permission: meal photo scanning, AI food lookup, and custom activity estimation send data to OpenAI or Exa only after you allow it in the app, and only when you use the feature. You can withdraw permission anytime in Profile → AI & Privacy.

One deliberate exception: if you've contributed a food item to BlobFit's shared community food database (via manual food entry), that entry stays searchable for other users after you delete your account — since someone else may already be relying on it — but it's stripped of any link back to you. Nothing else is retained.

6.Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so the app can function (showing your history, computing trends, etc.). If you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately and permanently, with the single shared-food-database exception described above.

7.Children's Privacy

BlobFit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8.International Users

BlobFit is available worldwide via the App Store. Wherever you're located, your data is processed as described in this policy. If you're in the EU/EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under GDPR or CCPA (such as data portability or a formal right to erasure) — the account deletion feature above satisfies the erasure request in practice; contact us for anything beyond that.

9.Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a way that meaningfully affects how your data is handled, we'll update the effective date above and, where practical, let you know inside the app.
